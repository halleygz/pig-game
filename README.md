# 🎲 Pig Game

A fun and interactive two-player dice rolling game built with vanilla HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

## Game Rules

**Objective:** Be the first player to reach 100 points!

**How to Play:**
1. **Roll the Dice** - Click the "Roll dice" button to roll a random dice (1-6)
2. **Accumulate Points** - Each roll adds to your current round score
3. **Important:** If you roll a 1, your current round score is lost and it becomes the other player's turn
4. **Hold** - Click "Hold" to safely add your current round score to your total score and pass to the next player
5. **Win** - First player to reach 100 points wins the game!

## Features

- 🎮 Smooth, interactive gameplay
- 🎨 Beautiful gradient UI with glassmorphism effects
- 🎲 Animated dice rolls with visual feedback
- 📱 Responsive design
- ⚡ Real-time score updates
- 🔄 One-click game reset

## How to Play

1. Open `index.html` in your web browser
2. Player 1 goes first (highlighted player)
3. Make strategic decisions: roll again for more points or hold to secure your score
4. First to 100 wins!

## Strategy Tips

- Rolling multiple times increases your risk of getting a 1
- Holding early locks in safe points
- Balance risk and reward to outsmart your opponent
